What We Do

Our Activities

Three integrated, self-reinforcing activities that create a circular food and income system for Mahama Camp families.

01
Core Activity

Agricultural Cultivation

Our main plantation grows a diverse range of fast-growing, nutritious crops using climate-smart methods. Production ensures a steady supply of fresh food to households, local markets, schools, and our centralized processing facility.

  • Leafy vegetables — kale, spinach, amaranth
  • Tree tomato (Tamarillo) — for Imbuto Splash
  • Root vegetables — carrots, beetroot
  • Herbs and legumes for soil health & nutrition
  • Organic composting and seed saving programs

Farming Principles

  • Climate-smart & eco-friendly methods
  • Water-efficient drip irrigation
  • No synthetic pesticides or fertilizers
  • Year-round continuous harvest planning
  • Seed saving and local variety preservation

02
Core Activity

Home Garden Installation

We design and install customized, space-efficient farming systems for individual refugee households. Every family receives a garden tailored to their available space and needs, with full training and ongoing support.

  • Raised beds for optimized soil depth
  • Vertical gardens for wall & fence spaces
  • Compost bins for organic waste recycling
  • Simple gravity-fed irrigation systems
  • Household training and follow-up visits

Installation Process

  • Household space & needs assessment
  • Custom garden design & materials
  • Professional installation by our team
  • Training session for family members
  • Ongoing monitoring & technical support

03
Value-Added Activity

Food Processing

Processing is centrally managed by the Imbuto team to ensure consistent quality, food safety, and regulatory compliance. Our flagship product — Imbuto Splash — is a 100% natural, stevia-sweetened juice made from tree tomatoes and beetroot.

  • Imbuto Splash juice — 330ml bottles
  • Packaged fresh produce — pre-washed, portioned
  • Dried vegetables & herbs (future phase)
  • Rwanda FDA compliant production
  • Full traceability farm-to-product

Product Standards

  • 100% Natural ingredients — no additives
  • Stevia sweetened — low sugar
  • Cold-press processing retained nutrients
  • Food safety & hygiene protocols enforced
  • Eco-friendly recyclable packaging
